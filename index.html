How to play

There is a website that uses AI to rate thousands of movies on how woke they are, from 0 to 100. On purpose. With a straight face.

Every day you get 5 movies. Guess the score each one got. To win, think like a guy who left a one-star review of Paddington 2.

  • Up to 1,000 points for the score. The closer you are, the more you get.
  • Then pick what set them off the most, from the site’s own categories, for a +250 bonus.
  • Be 40 or more off and you get nothing. You simply aren’t mad enough.
  • A new batch of grievances drops at midnight, your local time.

Higher means wokier, which to them means worse. Scores come from Woke Movie Score.
